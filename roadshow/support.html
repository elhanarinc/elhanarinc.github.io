FAQ

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open iPhone Settings → tap your name at the top → Subscriptions → Roadshow → Cancel Subscription.

How do I restore a previous subscription?

Open Roadshow → Settings → tap "Restore Purchases". Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID that purchased it.

Why isn't my Pro subscription active?

Try Restore Purchases first. If that fails, force-quit the app and reopen. If still inactive after 5 minutes, email us with your transaction ID (Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Roadshow → tap the subscription).

Is there a free trial?

Roadshow offers no free trial.

Is my data private?

Yes. Roadshow has no accounts and no third-party tracking. See Privacy Policy for the full breakdown.
