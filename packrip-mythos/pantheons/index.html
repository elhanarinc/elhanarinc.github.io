Server-driven rollout. Each pantheon's unlock level, card count, themed quests, and pack art live in Cloudflare Workers KV. New pantheons go live without an app update. The launch build ships with bundled offline fallbacks for Olympus and Asgard so the app works on a brand-new install with no network.

Why ten, why these

Ten is the right number to hit two design targets at once: a year-plus of content for a single solo dev to ship sustainably, and enough thematic range to keep the rip surprising. The selection deliberately stays inside public-domain material — no Hindu deities, Buddhist figures, Indigenous American spirits, African traditional religions, Aboriginal Dreamtime, named Abrahamic figures, or modern fictional IP like Slenderman or SCP. That's a content-safety choice, not a creative limitation: the included pantheons cover roughly 4,000 years of folklore.

Each pantheon ships

  • 60 creatures following a fixed rarity split: 25 Common, 15 Uncommon, 10 Rare, 5 Holo Rare, 3 Holo EX, 1 Shining, 1 Gold Star.
  • One pack art in the painted-mythology style.
  • One Secret variant reserved for the pantheon's flagship deity.
  • Themed quests — daily, weekly, and a super quest. Quests are KV-driven and can roll across multiple pantheons.
  • Pack-pull rules identical across pantheons so the rip feels consistent.
