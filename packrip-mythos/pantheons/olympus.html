What you'll rip in Olympus

The set runs the spine of Greek myth from the primordial generation through the Olympians. The fixed rarity split — 25 Common, 15 Uncommon, 10 Rare, 5 Holo Rare, 3 Holo EX, 1 Shining, 1 Gold Star — gives Olympus the same backbone as every other pantheon, but the casting choices are pantheon-specific.

Highlight chase cards

  • Apollo — Holo EX. Solar type. The set's poster card, gold-leaf radiance with a lyre motif on the holo. Hero of the launch screenshots.
  • Zeus — Secret variant. Storm type. The pantheon's flagship Secret pull (~5% rate), reserved for the king-of-gods slot.
  • Athena — Gold Star. Spectral type. The rarest standard-tier pull at ~2.8%.
  • Hades — Shining. Void type. Deep-violet holo with the helm of darkness.
  • Cerberus — Holo Rare. Bone type. The most-pulled holo in early-game packs.
  • Cronus — Holo EX. Titan type. Senior generation; pairs thematically with Saturnine cards in future Bestiarum.

How the artwork was made

Every Olympus card is a one-of-one render from a hand-written prompt sent to fal.ai's Flux model in oil-painting style. Filler tiers (Common to Rare) were generated with the faster Flux Schnell; the upper tiers (Holo Rare and above) used Flux Dev for tighter detail. The full pantheon cost roughly $0.33 in fal.ai compute. After generation each render is bundled into the app — no images stream from a server during pack opening, which is why the rip animation stays smooth offline.

Public-domain source material only. Olympus draws on Hesiod's Theogony, Homeric hymns, Ovid's Metamorphoses, and other public-domain sources. No art is referenced from contemporary illustrators, no figure is taken from a copyrighted franchise. The renders are interpretations, not reproductions.

Pantheon quests

Olympus ships with themed quests in the live quests.json config:

  • Daily — open packs from Olympus.
  • Weekly — pull a Secret-tier card from any Greek pack.
  • Pantheon Collector — complete the full 60-card Olympus collection (super quest, large XP+coin reward).

How to start ripping Olympus

  1. Install PackRip: Mythos from the App Store.
  2. Tap a pack on the home screen — Olympus is unlocked from level 1, no progression required.
  3. Use your daily free Basic packs first (5/day, 8 with PackRip Plus). Save coins for Premium if you're chasing a Secret or Crystal.
  4. If a specific card is missing late in the set, switch to a Hunt pack and target it directly.
