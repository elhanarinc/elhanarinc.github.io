Features

Tap to tear the foil. Swipe through cards one at a time, or jump to the grid summary. Real haptics on every reveal.

3D card tilt, rarity-driven shimmer, Crystal foils — all rendered live in SwiftUI. No Lottie, no canned video.

Target a specific card with rarity-weighted odds (8%–60%). Empty your wishlist on your terms.

🪙

Coin economy

Sell duplicates, buy Basic / Premium / Hunt packs. Daily streak rewards and per-level coin grants.

About the art

Painted, not photographed

Card art is AI-generated, offline. Every creature was prompted from public-domain mythology and folklore sources, rendered through fal.ai Flux in an oil-painting style, then bundled into the app. No assets are streamed at runtime, no card depicts a copyrighted character.
Indie, fan-made, no claim of ownership. All trademarks and sacred figures depicted remain the property of their respective cultures and traditions. Depictions here are artistic interpretations — not endorsements, not canonical renderings, not a religious work.
Pull-rate odds are disclosed in-app. The full rates table is one tap away from any purchase screen, per Apple's loot-box guidelines. Prices, pull rates and Plus perks are server-tunable and reflected in the disclosure sheet automatically.
