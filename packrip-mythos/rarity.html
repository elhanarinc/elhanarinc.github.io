Premium pack reweighting

Premium packs guarantee one upper-tier slot. There's a 5% chance that guaranteed slot upgrades from Holo to Secret. The remaining slots roll the standard distribution above.

Why disclosure matters

Apple's App Store Review Guideline 3.1.1 requires loot-box-style mechanisms to disclose odds before any purchase. PackRip's PullRatesSheet renders this table at runtime and is reachable from every pack purchase screen. Numbers stay in sync with the live config because the sheet reads from the same active* globals that the pack generator uses — there's no risk of marketing copy diverging from gameplay reality.

What rarity does not do here

  • It does not gate gameplay. There are no battles where a Crystal beats a Common.
  • It does not gate set completion. Every rarity is reachable on the free track with daily packs and patience.
  • It is not tradeable for cash, crypto, or any other transferable asset.

Rarity is exactly what it was when packs came in cardboard: a reason to keep ripping.
