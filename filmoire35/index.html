Film Stocks

Hand-tuned emulations of the strangest film ever sold

Each filter was prompt-engineered to evoke a specific film stock and camera body. Same filter on two photos never produces the same render.

CPM35 Expired Film

2000s · expired colour neg

Magenta and teal shifts, swollen halation, soft edges. The look of a roll forgotten in a glove box.

Disposable Flash

1990s · single-use

Edge-to-edge flash bloom, blown highlights, coarse ISO-800 grain, faint light leaks.

NT16 Natural Film

1980s · daylight neg

Soft, balanced tones — the everyday roll, dialed in for skin and skies without the drama.

Classic Noir B&W

timeless · monochrome

Rich black point, silver grain, deep contrast. Press-photo cinema in a single tap.

Instant Polaroid

1970s · integral

Warm cyan-magenta tint, soft sheen, slightly washed corners. A square memory of a Sunday.

Moody Low Contrast

muted · studio

Lifted blacks, gentle whites, desaturated mids. Editorial portrait energy.

Dreamy Film Pastel

2000s · pastel push

Bloomed highlights, mint-and-rose pastel cast, soft focus. A summer dream on print paper.

8mm Cine Still

1960s · super-8

Vignette, vertical scratches, organic 8 mm grain, subtle frame jitter feel.

Infrared Glow Film

surreal · IR-converted

White foliage, hot reds, dark skies. The "wrong" wavelength rendering of the world.

Heat-Damaged Stock

accidental · over-baked

Color crossovers, unstable density, tape-stripe artefacts. A roll that lived in a hot car.
