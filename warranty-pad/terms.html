2. The service

Warranty Pad lets you save items you have purchased, attach a receipt photo or PDF, and receive local reminders before the return window or warranty expires. Receipt text extraction (OCR) and field structuring run entirely on your iPhone using Apple's Vision and Foundation Models frameworks. Store presets and warranty defaults bundled with the app are heuristics maintained on a best-effort basis.

3. Informational only — not legal or consumer-rights advice

Warranty Pad is a personal reminder tool. The dates, return windows, and warranty durations it surfaces are informational. They are based on:

  • Heuristic OCR and parsing of your receipt
  • Default values you can edit before saving
  • Store presets that may be out of date or incorrect for your specific purchase

Always verify the actual return policy and warranty terms with the merchant before relying on a reminder. Warranty Pad does not provide legal, consumer-rights, financial, or insurance advice.
