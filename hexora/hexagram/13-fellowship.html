13. Fellowship

同人

Upper: Heaven  ·  Lower: Fire  ·  Pinyin: tóng rén

Judgment

Fellowship in the open brings success. Cross the great water through shared purpose. The superior person connects across differences.

Image

Heaven together with fire — the superior person organizes groups and distinguishes things by their nature.

Changing Lines

Line 1

Fellowship at the gate — broad connections bring no blame.

Line 2

Fellowship within the clan — narrow faction brings regret. Look beyond your inner circle.

Line 3

Hiding weapons in the thicket — for years without using them. Restrain aggression.

Line 4

Climbing a high wall — unable to advance, good fortune in retreat. Stop before conflict escalates.

Line 5

Comrades first weep and then laugh — after great struggle, they meet at the battlefield. True fellowship perseveres.

Line 6

Fellowship in the suburbs — no regret. Broad, inclusive fellowship that serves many.
