What is the I Ching?

The I Ching (易經, "Book of Changes") is one of the oldest works of divination in continuous use — a Chinese classic dating to around 1000 BCE. It maps every situation onto one of 64 hexagrams, six-line figures built from yin (broken) and yang (solid) lines. Each hexagram has a name, a judgment, an image, and guidance for any line that's changing — moving from yin to yang or vice versa. Read together, they describe the present moment and the direction it is leaning.

How to ask the I Ching online

The traditional method is the three-coin toss: each toss yields one line, repeated six times to build the hexagram bottom-up. Hexora animates this for you on iPhone — type your question, tap to cast, and the app shows the resulting hexagram with all changing lines highlighted. There is no login, no account, and no tracking. Your reading and any notes you add stay on the device.

What makes Hexora different

Hexora pairs the public-domain James Legge translation with a modern AI interpreter that grounds its response in the hexagram's judgment, image, and any changing lines, in the context of your specific question. The interpretation is poetic but practical — never medical, legal, or financial advice. Every reading is archived locally so you can return to it weeks later and notice patterns.

Daily oracle pull

If you don't have a question, Hexora offers a daily hexagram — a deterministic pull computed from your anonymous identifier and the date, so each day brings one reflection without you having to ask. Use it as a morning prompt, a journaling cue, or simply a reminder to slow down. Available in English and Turkish.
