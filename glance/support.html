Frequently Asked Questions

What is Glance?

Glance is a menu bar app for Mac that lets you track Polymarket smart money at a glance: watchlist markets, leaderboard wallets, and positions. Free tier includes 3 markets and 1 wallet; Glance Pro unlocks more and faster updates.

What are the free vs Pro limits?

Free: 3 markets on your watchlist, 1 tracked wallet from the leaderboard, and slower refresh. Glance Pro: unlimited markets and wallets, real-time WebSocket updates, and more frequent polling.

How do I add markets or wallets?

Use the "+" or "Add Market" to search and add markets to your watchlist. In the Leaderboard tab, click "Track" on a wallet to add it. You can remove items from watchlist or tracked wallets in Settings or via the remove (×) control on each row.

How do I open a Polymarket link?

Click on a market row or a position row; Glance opens the Polymarket event or market page in your default browser.

Where is the menu bar icon?

Glance runs from the menu bar (top right on Mac). Look for the eye icon. Click it to open the popover with Watchlist, Leaderboard, and Settings.

How do I quit Glance?

Click the menu bar icon, open Settings (gear icon), and use "Quit Glance" at the bottom.

Does Glance collect my data?

No. Glance does not collect, store, or transmit personal data. Market and leaderboard data comes from public Polymarket APIs. Your watchlist is stored only on your Mac. See our Privacy Policy for details.
