Captain Mittens: Cat Match 3

Rescue Kittens, Rebuild Town

A storm wrecked the little harbor town, and its kittens are trapped in cages all over the docks. Captain Mittens, the bravest orange tabby on the sea, needs a crew. That's you!

Coming soon to the App Store

What you do

  • Match and blast. Swap tiles to match 3 or more. Make 4, an L or T, a square or 5 in a row for special pieces, and combine them for huge combos.
  • Rescue the kittens. Break the cages and guide every kitten safely to the bottom of the board.
  • Rebuild the harbor. Every rescued kitten joins your crew and helps bring the town back to life.
