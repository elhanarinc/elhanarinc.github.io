Captain Mittens: Cat Match 3

A cat match 3 game for iPhone

Captain Mittens is a free, cat-themed match-3 puzzle game for iPhone. You match tiles, rescue kittens locked in cages and rebuild a harbour town wrecked by a storm.

Who it suits: short, relaxed sessions. Levels play offline, there is no account to create and ads are optional.

Who it does not: launch ships with 90 levels and two harbours, so if you want hundreds of levels today it is still small. It will grow with updates.

How it plays: match three or more, make a rocket, yarn bomb or seagull with bigger matches, then combine specials for huge combos.
