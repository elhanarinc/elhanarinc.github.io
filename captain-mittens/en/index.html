Captain Mittens: Cat Match 3

Rescue kittens, rebuild the town

A storm wrecked the harbour and locked the kittens in cages. Match tiles, break the cages and bring the town back, one match at a time.

Features

  • Match and blast

    Match three or more tiles, make specials like the rocket, yarn bomb and seagull, and chain combos.

  • Rebuild the harbour

    Earn stars, finish tasks and restore the storm-hit Liman harbour, Tuzla (the Salt Works), Bozcaada, Kaş and Amasra step by step.

  • 200 levels

    200 levels and five harbours, with new obstacles like barrels, rope and buoys to keep things fresh. More arrive in regular updates.

  • Cats to rescue

    Every cage holds a kitten; every kitten you free joins the crew.

  • Daily rewards and weekly ranking

    Collect a reward every day and climb the weekly ranking.

  • No account, offline levels

    No sign-up. Levels play offline; the weekly ranking, progress backup and rewarded ads need a connection.

Frequently asked questions

Is Captain Mittens free?

Yes, it will be free to download from the App Store. It has optional rewarded ads and optional in-app purchases; you never have to pay to play.

Can I play without internet?

Yes, levels play offline. The weekly ranking, progress backup and rewarded ads need an internet connection.

How many levels are there?

200 levels and five harbours (Liman, Tuzla, Bozcaada, Kaş and Amasra). More arrive in regular updates.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up or login. Your progress is saved on your device and backed up under a random Device ID (Settings → About → Device ID).

How do I delete my data?

Email elhanarinc@gmail.com with your Device ID and we will delete it. See the Privacy Policy and the Support page for details.
