In-app purchases#

The App offers consumable coin packs and an auto-renewing subscription called PackRip Plus. Every purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store, and payment details never reach us.

  • Prices are shown on the App Store listing and in the App before you confirm anything, and they vary by country. No price is quoted on this website, so nothing here can be out of date.
  • Coin packs are one-time purchases. Coins are consumed as you use them and are refundable only through Apple’s normal process.
  • PackRip Plus renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it in iOS Settings, under your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the renewal and you keep the benefits you already paid for until that period ends.
  • Plus benefits are listed in the App on the PackRip Plus screen, which is the current and authoritative list. They may change over time, and the App shows what you are buying before you buy it.
  • Refunds are decided by Apple, at reportaproblem.apple.com. Your access is not restricted because Apple granted one.

Randomised packs and odds disclosure#

Packs are randomised, and the odds are disclosed in the App before any purchase, in line with Apple’s App Store Review Guideline 3.1.1. The in-app Pull Rates screen renders from the same live configuration the pack generator uses and is the authoritative source; the field guide on this site explains the system but deliberately quotes no rates. Pack opening is entertainment. It is not gambling: there is no wager, no payout and no cash-out.
