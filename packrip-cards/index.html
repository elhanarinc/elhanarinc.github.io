The rip, natively

A real swipe tears the foil, cards turn over one at a time, haptics land under your thumb, and the shine follows the way you tilt the phone.

Frames from the App Store listing for the iPhone app, which ships PackRip’s own original card art. The app shows the live rates for each pack before you open it.

Memory Rip

A pairs game played with real cards from a set. Whatever you match, you keep.

  • A 20-card board and 10 moves. Flip two cards per move.
  • One rarer pair is hidden somewhere on every board, and the game shows its odds before you start.
  • Matched pairs go into a Prize Chest, which opens into your collection when the game ends. Chest cards never use a free pack.
  • One free game a day, two with PackRip Plus. After that, a game costs in-game coins.
  • Ten more moves, once per game, for an optional video or for coins.

The board: 20 cards face down. The violet pair marks where a rarer pair could be hidden.

Systems for people who finish sets

Pulling is the easy part. Closing a binder is the game, and all of this ships in the app today.

Hunt Packs

Heart the cards you are missing. A Hunt Pack targets one of them with up to 60% odds, and the app shows the exact chance before you spend.

Pity guarantees

Dry streaks are counted per protected rarity, with a visible counter, so you can see how close the next guaranteed pull is.

The Forge

Scrap duplicates into shards, then craft the exact card the set is missing.

Set checklists

Owned, Missing and Foils filters, completion bars, and coin rewards at milestones.

Seals, quests, streaks

Sixteen Seals with permanent perks, daily, weekly and Super quests, and a daily streak that pays coins.

Your language, your reminders

English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil) and Dutch, chosen in Settings.

No notification prompt on first launch, at most three reminders a day, and every channel can be switched off.
