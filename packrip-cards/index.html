What's in a pack

Eleven cards: one rare slot, three uncommons, five commons, two energy. The rare slot can roll Holo Rare, Holo EX, Rare Secret, Shining, Gold Star, or Crystal — and any pulled card can foil-roll independently.

✦

9 rarity tiers

Common, Uncommon, Rare, Holo Rare, Holo EX, Rare Secret, Shining, Gold Star, Crystal. Plus the elusive God Pack — eleven holos in a single rip.

Foil variants

Every card has a 1–5% chance to pull as a foil. Separate collection slot, animated rainbow stroke, brighter shimmer. Sells for 5× the base.

What's not in PackRip

A short list — sometimes the omissions are the design.

  • No ads. Not banner, not interstitial, not rewarded video. Nowhere.
  • No signup. No email, no Apple ID linking, no anything. Anonymous device UUID, that's it.
  • No PvP. Single-player collection loop. No matchmaking, no leaderboards, no ladders.
  • No third-party tracking. Standard Apple frameworks plus a tiny private Cloudflare backend for cloud save. That's the whole footprint.
  • No real-money trading. Pulled cards stay yours. No marketplace, no card-to-card transactions.
