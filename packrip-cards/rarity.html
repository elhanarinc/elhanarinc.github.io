Foil variants

Every pulled card has an independent foil roll after rarity is decided. Foils live in a separate collection slot, have a brighter shimmer and animated rainbow border, and sell for 5× the base price. They never replace your normal pull — they're a layered bonus chance on top of it.

RarityFoil chance per card
Common · Uncommon1%
Rare1.5%
Holo Rare2%
Holo EX3%
Rare Secret · Shining · Crystal · Gold Star5%

Pity guarantees

PackRip tracks how many packs you've opened without pulling each rarity. Cross the threshold for a given rarity and the next pack force-rolls it. The live counter is visible in the in-app Pull Rates sheet ("Pity — Guaranteed Pulls"), so you always know how close you are.

RarityGuaranteed after
Holo Rare20 packs without one
Holo EX50 packs
Rare Secret80 packs
Shining · Gold Star150 packs
Crystal200 packs

Pity is purely protective — it never reduces your odds and never costs anything extra. A god pack also resets the Holo / Holo EX / Rare Secret counters as a bonus, since it satisfies those tiers.

Premium pack reweighting

Premium packs guarantee one upper-tier slot. There's a 5% chance that guaranteed slot upgrades from Holo to Secret. The remaining slots roll the standard distribution above.

Why disclosure matters

Apple's App Store Review Guideline 3.1.1 requires loot-box-style mechanisms to disclose odds before any purchase. PackRip's Pull Rates & Odds sheet renders this table at runtime and is reachable from every pack purchase screen. Numbers stay in sync with the live config because the sheet reads from the same server-driven values that the pack generator uses — there's no risk of marketing copy diverging from gameplay reality.

What rarity does not do here

  • It does not gate gameplay. There are no battles where a Crystal beats a Common.
  • It does not gate set completion. Every rarity is reachable on the free track with daily packs and patience.
  • It is not tradeable for cash, crypto, or any other transferable asset.

Rarity is exactly what it was when packs came in cardboard: a reason to keep ripping.
