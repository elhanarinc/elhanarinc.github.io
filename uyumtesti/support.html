Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel my Uyum Testi Pro subscription?

Open iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, tap Uyum Testi, and choose Cancel Subscription. Your Pro access remains active until the end of the current billing period.

How do I restore a previous purchase?

If you reinstalled Uyum Testi or switched devices, open the paywall in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Your active Pro subscription tied to your Apple ID will be restored.

Why isn't my Pro subscription working?

First check that you are signed into the same Apple ID you used to purchase. Then tap Restore Purchases on the paywall. If the issue persists, email us with your transaction ID (visible in your Apple ID receipts).

How do I delete my data?

Your selfie is automatically deleted from fal.ai servers within 24 hours. To delete your local app data, simply delete the app from your iPhone. To request deletion of your server-side subscription record, email elhanarinc@gmail.com with your transaction ID.

How long is my selfie stored?

Your selfie is sent directly to fal.ai (an EU-hosted AI service) for analysis. The original image is permanently deleted from fal.ai servers within 24 hours. We never store your selfie on our own servers. The analysis result (type scores) is returned to your device only.

Where can I read the privacy policy?

The full Privacy Policy is available at /uyumtesti/privacy.html.

Purchases and refunds

All purchases are handled by Apple through your Apple ID. Manage subscriptions in iOS Settings under your Apple ID, then Subscriptions. Refund requests must be submitted through reportaproblem.apple.com. Uyum Testi cannot issue refunds directly.
